    Value of the day · 2 min
    The % reminds us: no one is left behind.

    Remember Rashid's buses in the Think deeper activity: 45 boys and 20 seats per bus.
    45 // 20 = 2 full buses carry 40 boys, and 45 % 20 = 5 boys are left without a seat.

    Those 5 boys still need a seat, so we book 3 buses, not 2. The % found the boys we would have left behind. Fairness means planning for everyone.
